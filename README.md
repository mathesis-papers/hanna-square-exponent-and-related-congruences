# Proof of Hanna's square-exponent congruence and an extension of Fried's divisor-sum formula

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

For Paul D. Hanna's conjecture in OEIS A226838, revision 14, FORMULA, we prove that every factorial-normalized coefficient of exp(sum_{m>=1} x^(m^2)/m^m) is an integer congruent to one modulo six by Carlitz's coefficient recurrence, and extend Sela Fried's Theorem 3 (2025, page 3) on Yanev's divisor-sum identity to all positive exponents. The same argument proves the modulo-three and modulo-thirty conjectures in A226839 and A226890, with all three uniform moduli optimal. A necessary and sufficient coefficient criterion gives a sharp weighted extension; the linked divisor-sum entry's elementary identities also follow.
